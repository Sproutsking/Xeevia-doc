01Engagement Points

Participation with weight.

EP is what makes engagement on Xeevia mean something. Engaging with someone's content, liking, commenting, replying, costs a small amount of EP. Posting is always free. It's not about gating the platform; it's about making sure engagement reflects real intent instead of empty noise.

EP is the operating economy of Xeevia, the one currency behind social activity, creator earnings and, as it develops, the advertising economy. When a conversation you took part in creates real value, you can share in that value, weighted by your reputation and the quality of what you contributed, not just how often you show up.

EarnComments earn their share

A good comment gets paid too.

A meaningful comment doesn't just add to a conversation, it earns its share of the value that conversation creates, instantly.

A comment doesn't draw from the post's total earnings, it generates its own. Every like and reply a comment earns is handled the moment it happens by a smart contract, which splits that engagement between the post owner and the comment owner on the spot. Nothing waits for a daily payout and nothing is pooled; every comment is settled on exactly what it earned.

  1. Someone likes or replies to your comment on a post.
  2. That engagement is routed to a smart contract the instant it happens, attributed to your comment alone.
  3. The contract splits it between the post owner and the comment owner, down to the last unit, on the spot.
  4. Both shares are credited immediately. There is no end-of-day settlement to wait for.
  5. Every comment is rewarded for precisely what it earned; no contribution is diluted across the conversation.

Not daily login rewards, not post-to-earn, not comment farming. EP rewards genuine contribution, down to the exact comment that earns it, and settles through a smart contract the moment it happens. Early-access settings, they may be refined as the economy matures.

02EP Wallet architecture

One balance. Four purposes.

EP is organized the way a well-run ledger separates balances by purpose without creating separate currencies. You hold one Xeevia EP balance, structured underneath into a small, intentional set of account purposes.

Available EP

The active balance used for everyday participation, likes, comments, boosts, community activity.

Creator EP

Earnings your network generates, campaign revenue, network rewards.

Savings EP

EP you deliberately set aside as a personal reserve for future participation.

Locked EP

EP committed to an outcome until it resolves, campaign escrow, conditional rewards, pending settlement.

You move EP between purposes with a single action: transfer. Creator EP can move into Available or Savings EP. Available EP can move into Savings EP. Locked EP releases into Available EP once its condition resolves.

EP is designed to be created inside Xeevia, circulate inside Xeevia, and be retired inside Xeevia, value staying inside the system that generated it. Any rules about moving value in or out of the platform will be stated clearly before they apply.

01Beneath the app

What Xeevia actually is.

Why build something deeper than a social platform? Because a person's digital existence is scattered, and every time it matters, it has to be pieced back together by hand.

Xeevia gives people a place to participate socially while building toward a more connected and verifiable representation of their digital existence, connecting identity, activity, records and evidence, with the source of each kept in view.

Xeevia is not primarily an advertising company, not primarily a token company, and not a platform built to extract the economic value of people's relationships. Advertising, EP, reputation and its other mechanisms exist to serve one deeper aim: a social and economic network built around genuine participation.

Not a follower-import tool. Not an all-knowing authority on truth. A social platform with a deeper direction, one that treats the original sources of records as the authority they are.

08Demand meets network

The Xeevia advertising economy.

Planned

Advertising becomes one market built on top of the network infrastructure, not the definition of the platform. A business with a legitimate need enters the Ad Centre; the Ad Engine finds qualified creator networks.

Ad Centre

Advertiser defines the demand

Budget, location and radius, audience and interest, campaign duration, maximum creator count and campaign objective.

Ad Engine

System evaluates the network

Creator eligibility, location fit, network relevance, reputation and trust, historical performance, contribution and result measurement.

  1. A business or person with a legitimate need creates a campaign.
  2. The advertiser chooses budget, location, audience, timing and other requirements.
  3. The Ad Engine evaluates Xeevia's network.
  4. Eligible creators are identified according to campaign-specific qualification.
  5. The campaign runs through those creator networks.
  6. Xeevia measures the defined campaign results.
  7. The protocol commission is removed from the campaign budget first.
  8. What remains becomes the Ad Pool, and the Ad Pool is 100%.
  9. Each creator receives a share of that pool based on qualifying contribution.
09Closed, fair, by construction

03Identity Graph

Connected, not assumed.

Planned

Identity on Xeevia is broader than a biography or a single identity check. A person may have identities and records across social platforms, professional networks, developer accounts, institutions, employers, domains, wallets, projects and communities.

The Identity Graph is meant to connect these where there's a valid basis for doing so. But connecting an account doesn't automatically establish every claim attached to it. These are different kinds of evidence:

Weakest

User-declared connection

You say an account is yours.

Stronger

Proof of account control

You show you can act as that account.

Stronger

Institution-issued record

A record that originated from the institution itself.

Strongest

Authorized confirmation

Confirmation from a source with the standing to give it.

Identity providers such as Self may be useful sources within this system, but no single provider defines the whole Xeevia identity model. A relationship is never treated as verified simply because two entities were connected, its basis always stays open to inspection.

04Query & verification

Ask the Oracle. See the evidence.

Planned

How can someone outside check a record they didn't witness? By asking what the available evidence shows, and getting an answer that names its sources.

The XRC Oracle is only as strong as the records, sources, permissions and verification methods actually available to it. Where verification exists, it should explain what was checked, which source was used, when, and what the result actually establishes. Where it doesn't, it should say so. 05Remarks

Statements with standing.

Planned · permissions and history controls come first

Remarks go beyond ordinary social comments. They let people or organizations with a genuine relationship to a record make traceable statements about it.

An institution

Confirms a record it issued.

An employer

Comments on a relevant employment record.

A project participant

Provides context about a contribution.

An authorized statement is kept distinct from ordinary opinion, preserving who wrote it, the relationship that gives them authority, the record concerned and any available evidence. It won't be presented as a working verification system until the right controls exist.
