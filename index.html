01Engagement Points

EP is what makes engagement on Xeevia mean something. Every meaningful action — liking, commenting, replying, posting — costs a small amount of EP. It's not about gating the platform; it's about making sure engagement reflects real intent instead of empty noise.

VISIONWhat Xeevia Is Becoming

The Xeevia
Vision.

Xeevia starts as a social platform. The vision goes much further — toward a singular source of truth beneath every platform on the internet.

Everything below is where we're headed, not everything you need to know to join today. Read as much or as little of this as you like.

01Beneath The App

What Xeevia
actually is.

Why does an identity layer even need to exist? Because every platform makes you prove yourself again, freezes what you built, and owns the people who chose to know you.

Xeevia is the layer underneath all of it — where your evidence, your trust, your reputation, and your relationships belong to you, and travel with you, no matter which application you're standing in today.

Not a blockchain platform. Not an identity provider by itself. Not a follower-import tool. The infrastructure underneath the social platform you're already using.

02Why This Matters

The cost of
fragmented trust.

Why does the internet feel expensive to participate in, even when everything is free? Because evidence is fragmented — so trust has to be rebuilt from zero, every time.

Fragmented
Identity

An Instagram self, an X self, a LinkedIn self. None of them travel. You rebuild from nothing on every platform you join.

Fragmented
Truth

No single source of verifiable evidence. Facts and claims contradict each other with no way to reconcile them.

Fragmented
Reputation

Years of contribution on one platform become invisible everywhere else. Nothing compounds. Everything resets.

Fragmented
Communities

Groups with no portable economy, no treasury, no governance — living and dying by the platform that hosts them.

Fragmented
Opportunity

Hiring, investment, and commerce all require re-proving yourself from scratch, every time.

Emerging
The AI Trust Problem

AI systems have no reliable way to verify identity or evidence — inheriting the internet's fragmentation at scale.

DOCUMENTATIONHow It's Actually Built

The XRC
Protocol.

How does truth get written so it never has to be argued about again? Every event, cryptographically linked, the instant it happens.

XRC is the deterministic, off-chain evidence layer beneath all of Xeevia. Six record chains capture everything. One Oracle makes it queryable by any system in the world.

XTRC — transaction record written — 4ms XARC — identity claim confirmed — 3ms XCRC — content provenance resolved — 5ms

01Record Chains

Six chains.
One record of truth.

XTRC
Transaction Chain

Every transfer, contribution reward, and fee — permanently linked and fully accountable.

XERC
Engagement Chain

Signals, reactions, replies — weighted and verified. No synthetic engagement passes through.

XARC
Account Chain

Signup, verification, device binding, security events. Your identity history, immutable.

XCRC
Content Chain

Creation, edits, ownership, deletion. Proof of authorship for everything you make.

XPRC
Permission Chain

Role grants, revocations, moderation. Every authority decision on record, always.

XSRC
System Chain

Protocol upgrades, rule changes, policy deployments. No hidden governance, ever.
